# Creating and Using Playlist Templates in ProPresenter
If you use a similar flow of content week after week, ProPresenter makes it easy to streamline your setup with **Playlist Templates**. These templates let you pre-define the structure of your service—including headers, placeholders, and even presentations—so you can start every week with a consistent framework.

### How to Create a Playlist Template

**Create a New Playlist**

- - Go to the **File** menu and select **New Playlist**,  
    *or*
  - Click the **+ icon** in the **Library section** (top left) and choose **New Playlist**.

![Screenshot 2025-04-15 at 1.24.49 PM.png](/hc/article_attachments/40377194812691)

**Build Your Playlist Structure**

- In the **Playlist Pane** (bottom left), use the **+ icon** to add:

  - **Headers** – For section titles like “Worship,” “Message,” or “Announcements.”
  - **Placeholders** – For content you’ll add later.
  - **Presentations** – If there are recurring elements you use every week (like a welcome slide or scripture reading).

You can drag to rearrange, rename items, or delete anything to fit your specific weekly flow.

![Screenshot 2025-04-15 at 1.26.15 PM.png](/hc/article_attachments/40377248418067)

**Save as a Template**  
Once your playlist is fully set up:

- - **Right-click** the playlist in the top left panel.
  - Choose **Save as Playlist Template**.
  - Give it a name that makes it easy to recognize later.

![Screenshot 2025-04-15 at 1.42.32 PM.png](/hc/article_attachments/40377194823315)

### Using Your Playlist Template

When you’re ready to start a new week:

1. Click the **+ icon** in the Library section.
2. Choose **New Playlist from Template**.
3. Select your saved template from the list.
